# 2026 AMS Madison Summit \- Complete Program PDF

A consolidated, searchable PDF of the full **2026 AMS Madison Summit** program, including oral presentations, panels, events, and poster presentations across all participating conferences\.

The official online program is comprehensive but spread across an interactive schedule\. This repository provides the entire program in one document for easier browsing, searching, printing, and offline reference\.

## Download the program

[**Download the complete schedule PDF**](./2026_AMS_Madison_Summit_Complete_Schedule.pdf)

## What is included

- 190 sessions
- 1,014 scheduled presentation\-level items
- 366 poster presentations
- Complete author lists as displayed in the official program
- Presentation titles, times, session numbers, rooms, and conference tracks
- Session chairs, cochairs, submitters, moderators, and panelists when listed
- Summit events, breaks, receptions, panels, and other scheduled activities
- Clickable links from session titles to the corresponding Confex session pages
- Clickable links from presentation titles to the corresponding abstract pages
- PDF bookmarks organized by day and session
- Searchable text throughout

## Schedule coverage

The PDF covers all dates displayed in the summit calendar:

- Wednesday, July 29, 2026 \- Online Poster Showcase
- Sunday, August 2, 2026 \- Pre\-conference programming
- Monday, August 3 through Friday, August 7, 2026 \- Main summit program

Times are presented in Central Daylight Time \(CDT\), consistent with the Madison meeting schedule\.

The summit brings together the following AMS conferences and committees:

- 34th Conference on Weather Analysis and Forecasting
- 30th Conference on Numerical Weather Prediction
- Committee on Artificial Intelligence Applications to Environmental Science
- 32nd Conference on Severe Local Storms
- 25th Conference on Planned and Inadvertent Weather Modification
- 22nd Conference on Mountain Meteorology
- 17th Conference on Cloud Physics
- 17th Conference on Atmospheric Radiation
- Eighth Conference on Weather Warnings and Communication

## Using the PDF

- Select a presentation title to open its abstract page in the official online program\.
- Select a session heading to open the complete session page\.
- Use the PDF outline or bookmarks to move between days and sessions\.
- Search by author, affiliation, presentation title, topic, room, or session number\.

## Source and currency

The document was compiled from the [official AMS online program](https://ams.confex.com/ams/2026Summit/meetingapp.cgi) on **July 31, 2026**\.

The official program remains the authoritative source and may be updated after this PDF was generated\. Check the [2026 AMS Madison Summit website](https://www.ametsoc.org/ams/meetings-events/ams-meetings/2026-ams-madison-summit/) or the online program for cancellations, time changes, room changes, and late program revisions\.

## Notes on the compilation

- Author names and affiliations are reproduced as displayed in the online program; abbreviations and capitalization have generally been preserved\.
- Some scheduled items, such as breaks or receptions, do not have presentation\-level records or abstracts\.
- A small number of session\-level entries in the online program do not include individual presentation details\. These are identified in the PDF\.
- Abstract text is not reproduced in the PDF; presentation titles link to the official abstract pages instead\.
